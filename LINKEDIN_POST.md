# LinkedIn launch post

I built something I care deeply about—and today it is live.

Meet **RightMatch Namibia**, a trust-first peer-to-peer vehicle marketplace designed around how Namibia actually moves.

The idea is straightforward: help vehicle owners earn from cars that would otherwise sit idle, give renters a clearer way to find the right vehicle for real life and help small or growing businesses access work-ready transport without first buying a fleet.

The hard part was never simply listing cars. It was designing for trust in a transaction where risk can be reduced, but never honestly promised away.

I designed and built RightMatch from start to finish: research framing, product strategy, brand direction, UI/UX, frontend, backend, database, verification journeys, evidence architecture, pricing logic, production infrastructure and deployment.

The platform is designed to provide:

• meaningful people and vehicle verification
• clear owner–renter terms
• guided pickup and return evidence
• a dependable deal paper trail
• transparent pricing and fees
• privacy-conscious trust signals
• a dedicated early-access path for business transport needs

RightMatch is not positioned as the vehicle owner, renter or insurer. It is the verifiable platform layer that helps both sides make better-informed decisions and preserve accountability.

The public early-access preview is now live at:

**https://rightmatchnamibia.com**

I’m grateful to my project partner, **Johny Kahuva**, for being part of the RightMatch journey and shared vision. I’m also proud to say that I personally carried the product build from concept to live deployment.

This is only the controlled-pilot foundation. Payments, document verification and live bookings will activate only as their legal, insurance and operational safeguards are completed.

If you are a vehicle owner, renter, small-business operator, mobility partner—or simply interested in what African-built digital trust infrastructure can look like—I would love for you to explore it and join the early-access list.

Built in Namibia. Built with intention. Built to move.
